Pediatric Dentistry

Calm, playful visits for kids, from the first tooth to the teenage years

Overview

About The Service

A child’s first few dental visits shape how they feel about the dentist for years. So we keep them short, explain everything in words kids understand, and let them hold the tools before we use them. Parents can stay in the room the whole time. Our pediatric dentists see toddlers through teens, and most of our young patients are back for their next checkup without a fuss.

What We Offer in Pediatric Dentistry

  • Dental Checkups for Children

    A quick look at how teeth are growing, so we catch problems while they’re small

  • Fluoride Treatments

    A painless coating that strengthens enamel. Takes about a minute.

  • Dental Sealants

    A thin shield over the back teeth, where most childhood cavities start

  • Cavity Fillings for Kids

    Tooth-colored fillings, done gently, with numbing gel before any injection
