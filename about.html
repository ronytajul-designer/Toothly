About Toothly

A neighborhood clinic run by dentists who would rather explain than upsell

From the founder

Why I started Toothly

For years I watched patients put off care until a small problem became a painful one. Usually it wasn’t the treatment they feared. It was not knowing what would happen, or what it would cost.

So we built Toothly around two rules. We explain what we see before we touch anything. And you get a written price before treatment begins.

Most of our team has been here from the start. We know our patients by name, and we’d like to know you too.

Dr. Ethan Marquez, DDS

Founder and Clinical Director

Our values

What guides us

The goal we work toward, and the promises we hold ourselves to every day

Our goal

To be the dental clinic people recommend to friends without being asked

Our promises

  • Explain every finding and option in plain language
  • Give you a written price before any treatment starts
  • Make visits calm for children and nervous adults
  • Recommend only the treatment you actually need

Every visit

How we work

Four habits you’ll notice from your first visit

  • We listen first

    We ask about your goals and worries before we pick up a mirror

  • We do it properly

    Our dentists train every year and use materials made to last

  • No surprise bills

    Every option comes with a price and a timeline, in writing

  • Comfort on request

    Headphones, a blanket, a break halfway through. Just ask.
